# AI Portfolio Maturity Model

## Level 1 — Tutorial Portfolio
Projects mostly follow tutorials with limited independent changes.

## Level 2 — Builder Portfolio
Projects are implemented independently and solve small end-to-end problems.

## Level 3 — Engineering Portfolio
Projects include architecture, testing, APIs, evaluation, error handling, and deployment thinking.

## Level 4 — Enterprise Portfolio
Projects include security, governance, integrations, observability, cost, scale, and realistic business workflows.

## Level 5 — Leadership Portfolio
Projects show architecture decisions, trade-offs, measurable outcomes, reusable patterns, and executive communication.
